What's New in Indian Ethnic Wear

Indian ethnic wear is evolving — and the best part is, it is more comfortable and wearable than ever.

Contemporary lehengas with lighter fabrics and shorter dupattas are perfect for dancing the night away. Versatile blouses with clean necklines and adjustable fits can be paired with sarees, lehengas and even skirts.

Another big trend is pre-stitched sarees and dhoti drapes, which give you the elegance of a saree without the hassle of pleating. For daily wear, co-ord kurti sets, palazzos and straight pants are replacing heavy salwar suits.

Color palettes are also softening. Blush pinks, ivory, dusty rose, antique gold and deep maroon are ruling this season — exactly the hues we love at Nyravi Boutique. Whatever the trend, custom tailoring ensures it is made for you.
