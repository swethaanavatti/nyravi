The Nyravi Fit: Alterations at Your Doorstep

Your body changes. Your clothes should change with it.

A beautiful outfit can feel wrong after even a small weight change. Instead of storing it away, get it altered. At Nyravi Boutique, we offer waist adjustments, length changes, sleeve alterations, side-seam refits and zipper replacements — all done quickly.

For minor tweaks, we can often complete the work at your doorstep during delivery or a short visit. This saves you time and ensures the garment fits before you wear it. For bigger refits, we pick up the garment and return it finished.

Good tailoring is not just about creating new clothes; it is also about giving new life to the ones you already own. That is why we call it the Nyravi Fit.
